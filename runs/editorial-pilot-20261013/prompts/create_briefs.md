# Create three distinct editorial briefs

Read the snapshotted project brief and editorial policy, accepted decisions, and exact retained evidence versions. Use only accepted, date-eligible material whose quoted passage or transcript segment you can inspect. If the minimum evidence is missing, report the insufficiency to the calling component; do not invent a source, passage, or argument to fill a quota.

Create **exactly three** JSONL brief records with `piece_id` values `01`, `02`, and `03`. Each record must contain `piece_id`, `title`, `question`, `argument`, `synthesis`, `limitations`, and `claims`. Each claim must contain a stable `claim_id` (for example `01-c1`), `statement`, `type` (`reported_fact`, `source_claim`, or `inference`), and a nonempty `evidence` array. Each evidence reference must identify an `item_id`, exact `version_id`, and a locator: `{ "type": "text", "quote": "exact passage" }` for written evidence or `{ "type": "segment", "segment_id": "..." }` for audio/video. Do not fabricate quotations or segment IDs.

Piece 01 asks what changed recently in Suno's workflow and why that matters to independent creators. Separate the company's October Studio announcements from older general workflows described by musicians; do not claim those musicians tested the new features. Piece 02 compares what quality/control tests actually measured, their different methods, and their limits; avoid an unsupported overall v6 score. Piece 03 asks what a creator should verify before release and what evidence to watch next. Distinguish user inputs and contribution, Suno or partner claims, participant concerns, and the procedural status of investigations or suits; do not imply a new feature caused a dispute.

Give each piece a distinct central argument backed by at least three independent originating publishers. Ensure written RSS discoveries, YouTube, and podcast material all contribute cited evidence across the series. At least one podcast claim must use a real timestamped segment. In `synthesis`, explain the inference that connecting multiple sources supports and what those sources cannot show. In `limitations`, identify a meaningful constraint or real counterargument and any missing stakeholder voice. Return JSONL only.
