# What to check before releasing a Suno-assisted song

*Evidence as of 2026-10-14 UTC.*

**Production note:** A Google Vids script for an approximately four-minute video. The recurring four-bar melody is **hypothetical**; no audio or screen action represents a Suno test. Timing uses a conversational pace of about 130 spoken words per minute, with brief visual pauses.

## Story outline

1. A creator has a release candidate and needs to explain how it was made. Introduce the hypothetical melody. [03-c7]
2. Turn that question into a file-by-file contribution record. [03-c1]
3. Show why a mixed human and Suno workflow needs more than one label. [03-c2]
4. Before another upload, check the applicable agreement; an investigation raises a question but settles no rights. [03-c3] [03-c7]
5. Separate Suno’s training statement, a complaint’s allegations, and musicians’ unanswered compensation questions. [03-c4] [03-c5]
6. Check release channels, then watch Deezer’s planned filter without assuming its effect. Return to the melody with a decision the evidence supports. [03-c6] [03-c7]
