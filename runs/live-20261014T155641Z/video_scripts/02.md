# What Suno quality tests can actually tell a songwriter

*Evidence as of 2026-10-14 UTC.*

**Format:** Google Vids storyboard · approximately 4 minutes · conversational voiceover at about 135 words per minute. The four-note hook shown throughout is **hypothetical**; it is not a Suno test or audio sample.

## Story outline

1. A songwriter has a four-note idea and wants to know whether a Suno-assisted version is ready to release. The question gives the viewer a reason to examine the tests. [02-c7]
2. Export measurements offer one check: levels. The viewer learns what WaveDisco measured, then wants to know what the numbers leave out. [02-c1]
3. A format comparison looks promising but covers only two songs. The viewer now knows to check their own exports. [02-c2]
4. A spectral comparison raises a different sound question, with different songs and codecs in each group. The viewer sees why the tests cannot be combined into a quality score. [02-c3]
5. Two creators try to direct musical results. The viewer shifts from measurements to whether an output follows an idea. [02-c4] [02-c5]
6. A listener’s enjoyment and artifact concerns show why one reaction is insufficient. The viewer gets a separate listening check. [02-c6]
7. Return to the four-note hook: check levels, intention, and artifacts on the candidate track. The ending answers the opening question within the evidence’s limits. [02-c7]
