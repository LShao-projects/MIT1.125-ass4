# Piece 01 — What Suno’s Studio update changes for hands-on creators

*Evidence as of 2026-10-14 UTC.*

**Production format:** Six-scene Google Vids storyboard, approximately 4 minutes 28 seconds. Intended delivery: about 120–125 spoken words per minute. The recurring guitar idea is **hypothetical**, not a Suno test.

## Story outline

1. **A guitar idea will not sit where the creator wants it.** The viewer gets a concrete editing problem and a reason to watch. [01-c5]
2. **Suno enters the workflow.** The viewer learns that Studio offers clip arrangement controls. [01-c1]
3. **Timing becomes adjustable.** Announced downbeat detection and time stretching suggest possible ways to place clips. [01-c1] [01-c5]
4. **The transition becomes adjustable.** Fade curves offer another choice the creator would need to judge by ear. [01-c1] [01-c5]
5. **Practitioners show why editing matters.** Todd Edwards and Zemi describe starting with their own contributions and shaping the result. [01-c2] [01-c3] [01-c5]
6. **A guitar-based account brings the opening idea back.** Caleb Porzio’s account makes the workflow plausible, while leaving the update untested. [01-c4] [01-c5]
7. **The viewer gets a bounded answer.** The controls may fit this workflow; release readiness remains a question for the creator’s own material. [01-c5]

**Outline check:** The guitar idea opens and closes the story. The practitioner accounts explain why the controls might matter, while the ending keeps the lack of an October before-and-after test explicit.
