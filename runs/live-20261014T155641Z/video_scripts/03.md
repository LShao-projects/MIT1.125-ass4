# Piece 03 — Before You Upload, Know What You Are Contributing

*Evidence as of 2026-10-14 UTC.*

**Format:** Google Vids storyboard, approximately 4:15 at a conversational pace. **Recurring motif:** a clearly labeled hypothetical song idea called “Paper Boat.” No Suno audio or product footage is represented.

## Story outline

1. **A release decision appears.** The hypothetical creator has a chorus idea and a Suno-assisted version. The viewer stays to learn what to check before uploading either one. [03-c5]
2. **The creator separates contributions.** A file-by-file record makes their account of the work easier to explain. The viewer gets a concrete first step. [03-c1]
3. **The upload raises a different question.** Suno’s account of v6 training makes the treatment of submitted material relevant, without proving what happened to this song. [03-c2] [03-c5]
4. **Terms require a current check.** Italy’s investigation shows why contractual language deserves attention; it supplies no finding or individual clearance. [03-c3]
5. **Broader deals do not answer personal rights questions.** Reported gaps in artist payout information and pending allegations keep the viewer from treating promises or lawsuits as settled outcomes. [03-c4]
6. **Release brings a platform question.** Deezer’s planned filter gives the viewer something specific to monitor, while classification of this song remains unknown. [03-c6]
7. **The opening decision resolves.** Keep the contribution record, check applicable upload terms, and check the receiving platform’s current rules before deciding whether to release. [03-c5] [03-c6]
