# Is This Version Better for My Song? | Suno in Practice, Episode 2

“Better” can mean that a song follows your melody, that a vocal sounds finished, or that an export meets your technical needs. This episode compares what several creator tests measured—and what those tests cannot establish for every song.

Companion article: “What Suno quality tests can actually tell you” in Suno in Practice on Substack.

Sources discussed:
- WaveDisco, “Is Suno v6 Better Than v5.5? Here Is What the Numbers Actually Showed” (September 20, 2026). Three songs were regenerated across model versions; only two had both WAV and MP3 exports for the format comparison.
- ytyng, “Suno v6: generation results, and what Variety and Max Mode actually do” (September 25, 2026). Export and spectrum observations.
- On a Creative Journey, “Can You Direct Suno V6 In Your Lyrics?” (October 9, 2026). Creator demonstration and repeat-generation caveat.
- Andrea Olsen / Produce Like a Boss, “Making the same song in EVERY AI generator” (October 9, 2026). Voice-memo and melody assessment.

The illustrated A/B versions are fictional, not actual Suno outputs. These tests use different inputs and methods, so the episode does not give Suno an overall quality score. Narration uses a Tova AI voice. Evidence was checked for the October 13, 2026 research run.
